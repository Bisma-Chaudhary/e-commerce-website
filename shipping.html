Shipping & Delivery

We aim to provide a smooth and reliable delivery experience for all our customers.

Delivery Time

Orders are usually delivered within 3 to 7 business days. Delivery time may vary depending on the location.

Shipping Charges

A standard shipping charge of $5 is applied to each order.

Order Processing

Orders are processed after successful checkout. Customers will receive their order after it has been prepared and shipped.

Delivery Address

Please make sure your delivery address and contact information are correct before placing your order.

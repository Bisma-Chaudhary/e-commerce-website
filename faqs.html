Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders are usually delivered within 3 to 7 business days. Delivery time may vary depending on the location.

What is the shipping charge?

A standard shipping charge of $5 is applied to each order.

Can I return a product?

Yes. Eligible products can be returned within 7 days of receiving the order.

How can I contact you?

You can contact us through the Contact Us page or using the contact information provided there.

How can I become a seller?

Click the Become a Seller button and complete the seller registration form.
